# Spec — agent-first, human-aware

**Contract.** The agent is the operator: it drives and acts autonomously by
default. The human is a viewer who can *always* observe and *rarely* writes. Both
hold at once because every consequential action is a single kind of object — a
**job** — not a blocking call. Autonomy is the default; a human write is the
exception.

**Chosen.** One organizing principle that every other decision serves. Read-only
defaults, preflight, node-owned rule-sets, and auto recipes all exist to *reduce*
the number of human writes; ids, live output, control handles, terminal records,
and detachment all exist to make a job *observable and durable*.

**Rejected.** Human-in-the-loop by default — approval on every step makes the human
a bottleneck, not a supervisor. Interventions are minimized by *governance policy*,
never by asking more often.

**Escape hatch.** A specific recipe may opt into `approval = human` to force a
prompt every time; that is a per-recipe choice, not the system default.
