# Spec — a job listing says what each job runs

**Contract.** Every entry of the job list carries, beside its id, status,
scope and name: the **argv** it ran (or would run — the corrected one once an
approval edited it), the **directory** it runs in, the **verdict** it
resolved under, and **how long its current phase has lasted** in seconds
(waiting for a verdict, or since it was spawned). The directory is the
requested one while the job waits, the confined one once it runs. The phase
is counted by the daemon that holds the job — after a daemon restart, from
re-adoption — and reads 0 once the job has ended (when it ended is not
kept). A face listing a node's
jobs therefore needs one call, not one per job.

**Chosen.** Fields on the existing summary: reads are node-global already,
and the argv and directory are what the audit records anyway.

**Rejected.** A per-job "describe" call (a list of twenty jobs becomes
twenty-one round trips over the hub); the environment (it may hold secrets
and never leaves the node).

**Escape hatch.** A job whose argv must not be shown is a job that should
not carry secrets on its command line — which governed jobs already must
not.
