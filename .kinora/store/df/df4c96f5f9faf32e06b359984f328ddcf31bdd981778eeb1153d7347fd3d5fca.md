# Finding — a re-adopted job loses its environment

Seen reviewing `start_vouched`, 22 Oct. `persist` never stores a job's env,
and re-adoption sets it empty. A live job keeps the env it was spawned with,
and nothing respawns from the handle today, so nothing breaks — but anything
that later respawns a re-adopted job from its handle would start it without
the environment it was given (for a vouched worker pane: no lease, no node
name). Store the env (it may hold secrets — then not on disk, and the daemon
that composed it re-supplies it) or keep refusing respawn of such jobs.
