# Finding — pty descriptors are not close-on-exec, so jobs inherit each other's terminals

`openpty` returns descriptors without `O_CLOEXEC`, and nothing sets it, so a job
spawned while another terminal job's master or slave is open inherits it (seen
21 Oct: a stray held test job held other jobs' ptys open). An inherited slave
keeps another job's terminal from ever reading EOF; an inherited master lets a
job read another's screen. Set FD_CLOEXEC on every pty descriptor the daemon
and the holder keep.
