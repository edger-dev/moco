# Spec — `autostart` and `restart` are two fields, with two different actors

**Contract.** First-start policy and exit policy are **separate declared fields**:

| Field | Question it answers | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `autostart` | **When is it first started?** | `manual` (default) · `boot` · `session` |
| `restart` | **What happens when it exits?** | `never` · `on-failure` · `always` |

They compose freely: a node daemon is `boot` + `always`; a dev checker is `session`
+ `on-failure`; a one-shot is `manual` + `never`.

**Each trigger names its actor, and they are different components.**

- **`boot` is the daemon's job.** At startup the daemon loads the **system**
  manifest and starts every `boot` entry, honoring its restart policy thereafter.
- **`session` is the agent's job.** The agent runs an idempotent **ensure** at
  session start: list the current workspace, start any `session` entry not already
  running. Ensure never stops or restarts anything, so it is always safe to re-run.
- **Workspace manifests are deliberately not scanned at boot.** The daemon does not
  know every repo on the machine, and walking the filesystem for manifests is
  unbounded and surprising.

**Ensure skips silently; an explicit start reports why.** A job refused by a policy
gate (kino://d8ab06ac3b5401a93b7ce2bcd93e6f9dfa6fa4285044cc11116f4d05e3b3f1f5) or
failing closed at the trust gate
(kino://a457400202f392b8f3cb8c1266e0e7a8b4ca92ed3cff07e1fcd54e1952442226) is passed
over by `ensure` without noise — a different caller with a correctly different
behavior — while an explicit request always names the refusing gate. `ensure`
remains prompt-free in practice because a job the human has already seeded runs
under an auto-allow.

**Chosen.** Two orthogonal fields, each naming the component that acts on it. This
is the schema decision with the largest reach in the source brief, and naming the
*actor* is what made it clean: two different components, at two different times,
from two different sources of truth, do the starting.

**Rejected.** Overloading `restart = always` to also mean "start automatically" —
it conflates first-start with crash policy and makes "start once at boot, never
restart" inexpressible. Also rejected: a bare `autostart: bool`, which cannot
distinguish the two triggers and therefore hides the two actors. Also rejected:
boot-time filesystem discovery of workspace manifests.

**Escape hatch.** `manual` + `never` is the default pair, so a job wanting no
automatic behavior of either kind declares nothing. A more robust `session` trigger
— a session-start hook invoking a CLI entry point rather than an instruction the
agent is asked to follow — is an additive refinement, not a schema change.
